Privacy notice

Last updated: 14-October-2026 · Operated by THE TOMAR METHOD (“we”, “us”), Gurugram (H.R), India.

The short version

We built this to be private by default:

  • The free Monthly Money Check and the Monthly Money Map run entirely on your own device. Your figures are not uploaded, there are no accounts, and we use no advertising cookies.
  • We receive your information only if you choose to send it — and even then your numbers travel inside a scrambled “report code”, not as open text.
  • For a paid review we hold your report and details for a short time, then delete them. We keep for longer only what the law requires us to keep (payment and tax records).

Who this applies to

Adults (18 and over) in India who use our website and services.

What stays only on your device

Everything you type into the free calculator and the Money Map — the amounts, the results, the what-if sliders and the PDF — is created and held on your own device. Closing or reloading the page clears it. We never see any of it unless you decide to share it with us.

What we receive, and only when you choose to send it

(a) When you contact us about a review: your first name (if you entered one), the figures contained in your report code (income, grouped spending, loans, savings and so on, in encoded form), the goal you picked, and the contact details the messaging app shows us (for example, your WhatsApp number or Instagram handle).

(b) When you pay: we see the payment information visible to us in our own bank / UPI app — such as the payer name or UPI ID and the transaction reference — so we can match your payment. We never ask for or collect your card number, UPI PIN, OTP, bank password or any similar credential. Please never send them.

(c) What we create: the review we prepare for you, and our correspondence with you about it.

The report code, explained

The code (it begins with “TM1…”) is simply the numbers you entered, turned into a compact, scrambled form so that someone who glances at your message cannot read your details at a glance. It is encoded, not strongly encrypted — anyone who holds the full code could technically decode it — so please do not post it publicly. We decode it only to prepare your review.

Why we use your information

To prepare and deliver the budgeting review you asked for, to communicate with you about it, to verify your payment, to keep the business and tax records we are legally required to keep and to handle any dispute or grievance.

Our legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you choose to send us your details for a review. For financial records, we also process data to comply with our legal obligations under Indian law.

How long we keep it

  • Your review, report files and related chat records: kept for up to 6 months after we deliver your review (or after your last contact if no review goes ahead), then securely deleted. You may ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Payment and invoice / tax records: Indian tax law requires us to keep records of our transactions. We retain these for up to 8 years, after which they are deleted. This legal obligation overrides a deletion request for those specific records.
  • We keep the minimum necessary, and we do not retain your detailed budgeting figures beyond the periods above.

Who else is involved

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

  • Messages reach us through WhatsApp or Instagram (operated by Meta), which handle them under their own privacy policies.
  • Payments are made through UPI and your and our banks.
  • If used we may use a reputable email or file-storage provider to deliver your report, such a provider processes limited data on our behalf under its own terms.
  • We may disclose information where required by law or by a lawful authority.

Where it is processed

We operate from India and process data in India.

Security

Your figures stay on your device by design. What we do hold is kept on access-controlled devices and accounts protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication. No method is perfectly secure, so please share only what you are comfortable sharing, and never send passwords, PINs, OTPs or Aadhaar numbers.

Children

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Grievances

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact thetomarmethod@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge within 7 days and to resolve matters promptly.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the current version, and material changes will be highlighted on this page.

Educational use only

Our maps and reviews are educational cash-flow and budgeting summaries based only on the numbers you provide. They are not investment, tax, insurance or legal advice, and they recommend no security, fund, insurance policy or other product.
